## P1 Issues

- **The "mirror of regenerated_sections" claim borrows a safety argument that does not transfer** (flagged by architect, critic, independent-thinker, and analyst — the most broadly corroborated finding in the round). D-2 justifies excluding `regenerated_sections` because they are Category 2 views regenerated from structural content which is itself hash-verified, and D-5 floors them at max 10 sections at schema level and under 50 percent of source lines at runtime (`src/core/validate.ts:61`, bound at `:6` and `:21`, `IntegrityFloorError` at `src/adapters/plan.ts:375-376`). Scaffolding is authored in the plan, not derived from the source, and `ClusterScaffoldSchema` at `plan-yaml.ts:88-102` carries only minimums — min 1 tag, min 1 observation, min 1 relation — and no upper bound. Because `range` is optional and a legal range is as small as one blank line, a destination can be almost entirely unproven scaffolding while its byte proof passes vacuously — the exact abuse the 50 percent floor exists to prevent on the mirrored side. Analyst adds that "excluded from extraction" is vacuous for scaffolding, since scaffolding never exists in the source ("authored into the destination rather than stripped out of the source", `cluster-scaffold.ts:16-19`); the verified property is exclusion from hash comparison only (`decompose.ts:275-276`, `332-345`). Architect's recommended framing: state the asymmetry — derived-and-floored versus authored-and-verified-but-unbounded — instead of asserting a mirror.

- **Entry 2's root cause is named too narrowly: the generator is a second implementation of the whole schema** (flagged by architect). `shared/composition/schemas/` (D-5's canonical modular layout) and `src/schemas/plan-yaml.ts` (what `decompose.ts:45,380` loads) encode D-1 incompatibly — `source_path` plus top-level maps plus `clusters`, versus `sources[]` plus `destinations[]` plus per-destination `mutations`. Importing one validator leaves the drift generator intact, which is precisely why the CWE-22 P0 survived.

- **"Imported, never re-stated" over-reaches past its own evidence** (flagged by independent-thinker). The entry's first sentence is precise — the bug was a second *implementation* — but the closing imperative generalizes to enforcement *statements*, which collides with deliberate practice in this same library, where the same claim rules run both as schema `superRefine` at parse time and as standalone validator functions at runtime. The fix itself applied one implementation at four call sites across two schemas. Tighten to "one implementation, arbitrarily many call sites" so a future reviewer cannot cite this ADR to delete a legitimate second enforcement point.

- **The scaffold entry argues integrity but never states the trust boundary it opens** (flagged by security, condition 2). The excluded region is plan-controlled content written to disk and covered by no proof at all. `renderEpilogue` interpolates raw at `cluster-scaffold.ts:86` and `:90`, and `renderPrologue` re-emits the title raw into the H1 at `:71` after `yaml.dump` has already safely quoted it for the frontmatter block. The only gate is the schema, and `ObservationSchema.text`, `RelationSchema.target`, and `ScaffoldFrontmatter.title` are all `z.string().min(1)` (`src/schemas/common.ts:102,110`; `plan-yaml.ts:91`) — presence, not structure. A title or observation carrying a newline-delimited `---` or `## Relations` injects a second frontmatter-shaped block or a duplicate final section into the destination note, and because `stripScaffold` re-derives the *same* poisoned prologue and epilogue, the strip succeeds and the hash comparison passes over an untouched body. The proof stays honest about the content slice and blind to the injection. Since the plan is already treated as untrusted for CWE-502 and CWE-22, treating it as trusted for rendered content is an inconsistency the entry should record.

- **The `regenerated_sections` floor is unreachable from the CLI, and the entry does not say so** (flagged by critic). `DistributionPlanSchema` is `.strict()` with no such field, and the executor's `Mutations` type is `{renumber_map, wikilink_map}` only at `decompose.ts:112`, so the 50 percent integrity floor at `core/validate.ts:34-61` and D-2's `frontmatter_map` contract cannot be exercised by any plan the CLI accepts. A reader of D-2 and D-5 will assume the opposite.

- **The strip-verification failure mode is named but not classified** (flagged by critic). A failed `stripScaffold` throws `integrityError` leading to exit code 2 with nothing renamed (`decompose.ts:340-343` and `:114-119`) — BLOCKING, not advisory. "Verified at strip time" alone would let an implementer log a warning and continue.

- **Entry 1 omits the schema rule that makes retention usable** (flagged by critic). A `retain` cluster must not declare `destination_path` or `scaffold`; both are rejected by `superRefine` at `plan-yaml.ts:142-162`. The rule is present in the decompose skill doc but absent from the ADR.

- **The generalized drift lesson is filed where it binds nothing** (flagged by high-level-advisor). "Guards with a canonical home must be imported, never re-stated" is a governance rule; inside a single component ADR it binds nothing and is undiscoverable from the next component that needs it. Its home is the ecosystem surface where F-8 and the BLOCKING-gate concept already live. Leave the concrete instance here, where it belongs.

- **Zero Observations were added for two facts called architectural** (flagged by high-level-advisor). Observations are the search and graph surface; recorded only in Clarifications prose, these facts are durable in name only. Advisor also recommends splitting entry 1, on the grounds that it bundles two independent facts and the one with teeth — retention bounding reversibility — is buried in the second sentence behind the benign one.
