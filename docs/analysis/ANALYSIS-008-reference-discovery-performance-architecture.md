# ANALYSIS-008: Reference-Discovery Performance Architecture

## TL;DR

Three-wave deep research (8 investigator cells, 4 red-team cells, 3 measurement cells, 4 recon scouts) into the fastest RELIABLE architecture for complete multi-pattern reference discovery over the local SQLite-backed markdown knowledge graphs. Core answers:

1. **The cost was never the proof and never the matcher.** ~96 percent of the exhaustive leg's ~207ms per-call cost is incidental SQL machinery that full-scans all 46,310 rows of the whole 17-project store three times per call, because the FTS5 virtual table declares its filter columns UNINDEXED. The freshness proof (tree walk) costs ~1ms; the matcher choice is worth under 7 percent of latency.
2. **The proof spine of any honest design is: walk + read + hash-verify.** The stat-walk alone is a demonstrated FALSE proof (a preserved-mtime edit passes it; the ~0.65ms whole-corpus Bun.hash verify catches it). Both the current leg and its sibling references leg serve `provable: true` over stale indexed content — a live defect with four fond notes measurably stale at capture time.
3. **On top of that spine, a REGULAR (content-storing) trigram FTS5 table beats the N-pass scan 1.83-4.28x under equal proof standards** — Wave 1's FTS5 disqualification was an artifact of testing the external-content configuration. The regular table's desync surface does not exist (0 of 5 normal write paths, corruption trap structurally unreachable). Its honest costs: 3.3x storage, size-dependent reindex (1.2ms + 0.134ms/KB), an N-dependent write-amplification crossover (fond at N=30 is past it; at N=5 it is not), and a mandatory sub-3-character guard (or use LIKE, exact at all lengths, 2.9x MATCH cost).
4. **The request-scoped shape banks nearly all of the win with none of the cross-call hazards.** Server-scoped warm state buys ~58ms over a ~78ms request-scoped baseline and purchases seven hazards, three of which break the proof class. The regimes where warm wins and where warm is affordable do not overlap.
5. **The ceiling fear retires.** 10,000 notes projects to 50-150MB (point ~85MB), not 495MB — that figure was a research-phase composition artifact (fond is 77 percent ANALYSIS notes; mature graphs add light notes: skills holds 5.3x the notes in 0.90x the bytes). The walk at 10k notes is 27-72ms (directory-dominated, 7.2x per-dir vs per-file), not ~594ms. Everything stays sub-second at every credible projection under every candidate.
6. **The largest LIVE correctness defect is not performance-related**: the scanner's file-level target exclusion drops 326 cross-target occurrences (272 in prose) when targets are batch-scanned — silent, unreportable by closure, growing with batch width. Validated fence-preserving fix: per-candidate filtering instead of file skipping.

## Decision Context

The owner directed a two-stage funnel for fast curation (CLI search identifies referencing notes at 100 percent recall; positional utilities scoped to those notes; compose utilities apply mechanical updates), then designated the performance question a deep-research topic before authorizing any server build: the incumbent candidate (server-side batched multi-needle SQL, measured 7.8x on SQL) competes against the field; incompatible-but-larger wins ship instead. Constraints throughout: surface parity between CLI and MCP; provability never traded for speed; no new dependency without a strong case; no backwards compatibility owed; the goal is frequent curation without paying a large price.

## Stakeholder Briefs

**For the owner (decision-maker).** The build that the evidence supports is a server-side rework of the two complete-retrieval legs around a self-proving request-scoped read (walk + read + hash-verify against stored digests), with matching served either by native containment (simplest, ~19ms at fond scale) or a regular trigram index (fastest, ~7ms, with storage and write-amplification costs that only pay off above small-batch curation). All candidate latencies are imperceptible at your scale and sub-second at any credible 10k-note future; the decisive differences are correctness properties, not speed. Three correctness fixes are independent of the architecture pick and are the highest-value items in this study: the cross-target exclusion fix, the stale-index currency fix, and the alias-declaration route for the known residual. One curation-practice lever dwarfs engineering at the ceiling: capping session mega-ledgers.

**For the implementing engineer.** The seam design in the shipped funnel is confirmed correct: runQuery as the sole invocation point absorbs any server-side batching as a local swap. The matcher ships as-is — the K1/K2 knobs are DO-NOT-SHIP (0 true recoveries, 435 false positives on real corpora). The 61-case adversarial fixture corpus from the research should be promoted to a test asset. Every measured number in this note carries its conditions in the Evidence Ledger and its harness in scratch/deep-research-cells/.

## Findings by sub-question

### F1 — Cost anatomy of the exhaustive leg (Q1, cell 1; confidence 0.93, triangulated)

Warm per-call median 181.4ms decomposes as: DB open 0.22, project lookup 0.51, containment match 61.5, COUNT(DISTINCT) 57.0, tree walk ~0.9, indexed-path set 57.3, unindexed scan 0.03. Stubbing the incidental statements leaves 61.4ms; a bare no-predicate scan costs 53.8ms of that, leaving ~6ms of true semantic work. Structural cause: `search_index` is an FTS5 virtual table with `project_id` and `type` UNINDEXED — every statement full-scans all rows of all projects (104.8MB shadow table; fond contributes 0.16 percent of rows). Fused single-scan form measured 62.65ms with machine-asserted output equivalence (matchPaths, indexedNotes, pathSet identical). Rowid-restricted floor 6.62ms with provably identical results (EXCEPT-verified) — reachable only with a project-to-rowid map that does not exist. The fixed cost scales with the whole store, not the queried corpus; the 1333ms cold figure is the page-cache-cold read of the shadow table. Q8 (same cell, 0.95): stage two requires docs-root-relative file_path identities and disk reads (which also feed frontmatter identity and the GRAPH leg); index approaches are disqualified only if they return opaque IDs without path columns.

### F2 — The completeness defects (Q5; cells 2, RT1, RT3; joint)

- **Cross-target exclusion (RT1 census + cell 2 independent reproduction; 0.95).** The TEXT scan excludes target notes at file level where the documented intent is self-citation. Batch-scanning 28 targets drops 326 cross-target occurrences (272 prose, 54 Relations; 27 of 28 target notes; 108 pairs). Repointing is addressed substitution and closure diffs prior findings, so the class is silently unrepairable and unreportable — the operation reports closed with stale references left behind. Grows ~N-squared with batch width; single-target repoints have zero exposure. Validated fix: per-candidate filter (candidate.target === own entity ID), which preserves the load-bearing suppressions (a note's own frontmatter title/permalink lines) and whose newly-surfaced Relations findings merge into the existing BOTH category.
- **Stale-index currency hole (cell 4 C2 inference, RT1 confirmation 0.97, RT2 independent corroboration).** Indexed files are never re-read; both legs serve `provable: true` over edited, appended, or deleted-on-disk content (T1-T5 battery on throwaway copies; references leg has zero filesystem calls and unconditional provable:true). Live at capture: 4 fond notes stale including the active session note (29 minutes / 5,833 bytes behind); zero wrong answers at that instant, one appended reference from exposure. The stronger proof is cheaper than the weaker one being paid: entity.checksum/mtime/size exist and a full currency sweep costs 0.92ms vs the 4.1ms-class membership walk. search_index.updated_at is unusable (100 percent false-stale rate).
- **Matcher knobs DO-NOT-SHIP (RT3; 0.90-0.95).** Case-insensitive bare-ID and permalink matching recover ZERO true references on the real corpora and introduce 435 false positives (git branch names, frontmatter tags, source filenames, cross-attributions inside other identifiers and inside wikilinks addressed elsewhere) plus 15 silent class corruptions. The fixture recoveries were corpus artifacts: agent-authored graphs are uniformly canonical. Falsification sweep at maximal breadth (every note a target) found zero genuine lowercase citations. The residual live exposure of all variant forms is 5 occurrences, 4 being the known ANALYSIS-028/033 alias residual — closed by declared aliasEntityIds, not matcher relaxation.
- **Pattern-enumeration recall is UNMEASURED (RT1 + cell 2 joint; 0.92).** All three circulating recall figures are blind to it for different structural reasons: 511/511 shares one matcher across both arms; funnel-recall's ground truth is matcher-derived; 316/316 is matcher-independent but near-tautological (containment vs containment) on a corpus containing zero variant forms as sole evidence. An honest end-to-end completeness claim requires an adversarial corpus; the 61-case fixture set is the seed.

### F3 — Architecture adjudication (Q3, Q4, Q6, Q7, Q9; cells 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, RT2, RT4, W3-2)

- **Proof spine (W3-2 C8, 0.85; cell 4; cell 7).** The stat-tuple walk alone is a demonstrated false proof (preserved-mtime edit passes; hash catches). The honest spine: enumerate (walk — additions/deletions), read, verify content digests (Bun.hash ~0.65ms per 3.3MB; ~7ms per 33MB). Event-driven invalidation is disqualified as proof on darwin (FSEvents ordering disclaimed by Watchman's own docs); eight production systems refuse channel-equivalence; the queue-drain pattern (obsidian-backlink-cache two-tier API) is the transferable design IF warm state ever returns, with proof cost O(edits) — but see topology.
- **Topology (cell 8; RT2).** Client-side in-process is structurally impossible (Go CLI, TS server) and REJECTED. The server is the single parity-free location for performance changes (schema-as-contract + 3 mechanical gates + 25 paired probes hold parity, matching LSP/protobuf precedent). Request-scoped state banks the batching win (~78ms pull+scan at fond scale) with today's proof class and closes the currency hole by construction; server-scoped warm state buys ~58ms more and purchases seven hazards including three proof-class breaks (symlink invisibility — probed; read-then-stat permanent staleness under the SDK's confirmed concurrent dispatch; orphaned DB handle serving a deleted database), ~2GB-per-instance ceiling memory under 16-bit string inflation (3.8x, 66 of 69 fond files non-ASCII), parity-invisible multi-instance divergence, and per-session death of stdio servers. Verdict: request-scoped now; warm only against RT2's seven conditions and the measured 58ms.
- **Matching engine (cell 6; W3-2).** The matcher question is near-moot at today's fixed costs (<7 percent of latency) but decides the equal-proof contest: regular trigram MATCH beats N-pass includes 1.83-4.28x at every N and both scales (N=30/3.3MB: 7.39 vs 18.90ms; N=30/33.4MB: 52.87 vs 191.42ms; walk+read+hash included in both legs). Regular-table desync surface does not exist (9-path battery; cell 3's trap rejected outright; shadow writes blocked by defensive mode — Apple SQLite verified, Linux unverified). Costs: 3.3x storage; reindex 1.204ms + 0.134ms/KB (fond mean note 7.82ms — RT4's flat 12.23ms falsified); crossover is an N-dependent surface (break-even edits/query at 3.3MB: 0.41 at N=5, 1.47 at N=30; in corpus size at 1:1 ratio: 8.3MB at N=5, 2.46MB at N=30). Query surface: LIKE is exact at ALL needle lengths (indexed on regular trigram, 1.7x over no-index at this scale — the literature's 125x is a scale artifact) at 2.9x MATCH cost; MATCH requires a hard sub-3-character guard (silent-empty confirmed on every config). Best query shape at scale: regular FTS5 for content + plain side table for path/digest (22.89ms vs 78.14ms for UNINDEXED-column retrieval at 10x). JSC includes() mechanics: adaptive V8-ported searcher (SIMD anchor then Boyer-Moore after 16 failures) — throughput is skip-distance-driven, best on long identifier needles; Aho-Corasick loses 3.5x below ~100 needles natively too (Teddy corroboration); attribution is a universal two-stage tax; a native AC binding exists for Bun but fails the dependency bar (324 weekly downloads, 1 star). Dependency verdict: NONE.
- **Community practice (cell 7).** No comparable tool claims provable completeness; the only 100-percent-accurate claim in the field (obsidian-backlink-cache safe tier) is earned by queue-draining. Trigram-as-completeness is refused by both first-party precedents (basic-memory removed trigram variants; Logseq caps and bypasses). Upstream findings for the report-only ledger: the basic-memory tokenchars 0x2F config is a no-op (measured — hex is not accepted; slash never became a token character; permalink MATCH never did what the schema comment claims); count-based deletion detection has a delete-plus-add hole explaining stale index rows; measured FTS ceiling on the live corpus 34 of 37 notes for the Move-4 identifier (truncation component beneath the alias residual).

### F4 — The ceiling, measured (Q2; cell 5 model, W3-1 and W3-3 measurements)

- **Bytes (W3-3).** 10,000 notes projects to 50-150MB (point ~85MB; stress 192MB; 15 named models). The 495MB premise reproduces exactly as fond's research-phase mean times 10,000 and retires: fond is 76.8 percent ANALYSIS notes with zero task/qa/req/design notes; mature graphs add light notes (skills: 5.3x notes, 0.90x bytes; task/qa/req at 3.9-6.7KB means, 74.7 percent of count). Session-ledger hypothesis falsified for fond (median 86 percent of mean — composition artifact) but mega-ledgers are the dominant future lever: 18 percent of sessions hold 79 percent of session bytes; at fond's ledger habits session notes alone approach the central projection.
- **Walk (W3-1).** Directory-dominated: fitted 0.141ms + 2.63us/file + 18.94us/dir (R-squared 0.9997, two held-out validations within 7 percent). 10k-note ceiling: 27-72ms enumeration, 56-111ms with per-file stat. The 59us/note figure is not reproducible (worst constructed condition 1.465ms vs fenced 4.1ms — unresolved contradiction, honestly bounded); the 4.8us/note figure is approximately right. 58.9 percent of walk cost is the server's own path-normalization JS — the cheapest lever, no FFI needed (getattrlistbulk reachable but dominated).
- **Composite.** At the ~85MB point estimate: request-scoped pull+scan ~400-460ms; warm-scan-only ~230-280ms; regular-trigram equal-proof leg materially under both. Everything sub-second under every candidate at every credible projection; the index earns its keep on matching speed and loses on write amplification exactly as the N-dependent crossover surface predicts.

## Contested Points (adjudicated)

1. **Warm corpus vs request-scoped.** Adjudicated for request-scoped: the ~58ms marginal buys seven hazards, three proof-class breaks, and a memory/parity story that fails at exactly the scale where warm would matter. Warm remains a documented option behind RT2's seven conditions.
2. **Scan vs regular trigram.** Adjudicated as workload-dependent with measured surfaces: index wins matching 1.8-4.3x under equal proofs everywhere; scan wins total-work in small-N edit-heavy curation at fond scale (break-even 0.41 edits/query at N=5). Both imperceptible today. The synthesis recommends shipping the proof spine first (correct under either engine) and choosing the engine by measured workload (the real edit:query ratio is the named unknown).
3. **The fenced 4.1ms walk figure vs W3-1's 1.465ms ceiling.** Unresolved contradiction, conservative in direction (the walk is cheaper than fenced); candidate explanations recorded (single-sample capture, machine load, instrumentation overhead).
4. **"33MB across all projects" vs the 3.2GB database file.** Resolved: 33MB is indexed text volume; the file is dominated by vector-embedding tables (2.28GB) and FTS5 index structures the query path never touches (cells 1 and 8).
5. **Cell 3's external-content FTS5 disqualification vs W3-2's regular-table result.** Resolved: both correct for their configurations; the citable conclusion is configuration-scoped. Cell 3's LIKE figure (254.5ms) additionally did not exercise the trigram LIKE index (pass-through on external-content); indexed LIKE is real (EXPLAIN-verified L1) at 1.7x this scale.

## Open Questions (bounded, with falsification tests)

1. Real curation edit:query ratio — decides the engine crossover at fond scale. Measure over actual curation sessions.
2. 690 DISTINCT-note trigram behavior — the 10x corpus was near-duplicate copies; doclist selectivity on distinct notes is the largest threat to the 10x index numbers.
3. Linux defensive-mode — the shadow-write blocking is verified on Apple SQLite only; one PRAGMA probe on a Linux Bun settles it.
4. Pattern-enumeration recall — unmeasured by construction everywhere; requires the adversarial corpus (seeded by the 61-case fixture set).
5. Occurrence-level variant exposure beyond fond — the census covered fond; skills was covered only for the matcher-knob question.

## Recommendations (in dependency order)

1. **Fix the cross-target exclusion** (per-candidate filter; skills composition core; validated, fence-preserving). Precedes or accompanies any batch-width increase — batch width amplifies the defect.
2. **Fix the currency hole in both _brain legs** (walk upgraded to digest verification against entity.checksum or stored Bun.hash digests; re-read changed files as scanUnindexedFiles already does for unindexed ones; honest provable:false when verification fails). Closes RT1's T1-T5 battery and makes the walk prove currency, not membership.
3. **Rework the exhaustive leg to a request-scoped self-proving batched read** (single fused pass or corpus read; batched multi-literal arguments on both surfaces with per-token attribution and per-target references completeness — the engineer's designed response contract; runQuery-local client swap). Banks 77-96 percent of the available win with today's proof class.
4. **Engine choice on measured workload**: default to the batched containment scan (no storage cost, no write amplification, simplest); adopt the regular-trigram side-table shape when matching cost dominates measured curation time or the corpus passes the measured N-dependent crossover. If adopted: LIKE surface or hard sub-3 guard; side path/digest table; Linux defensive-mode probe first.
5. **No-regret fixes** (cell 8): busy_timeout on read paths; PRAGMA table_info feature-detection before first query; symlink policy in the walk; the path-normalization JS diet (58.9 percent of walk cost).
6. **Close Move 4 by declared aliases** (aliasEntityIds on the renumbered target) — confirmed as the correct lever by three cells; matcher stays as shipped.
7. **Promote the 61-case adversarial fixture corpus to a test asset**; adopt deterministic op-count complexity guards (cell 7's transferable testing technique).
8. **Curation practice**: cap session mega-ledgers (the dominant controllable byte lever at ceiling); the report-only upstream findings (tokenchars no-op, deletion-detection hole, stems truncation) join the owner's basic-memory ledger.

## Methodology Appendix

Exhaustive tier, deep-dive scope. Recon: 4 scouts (landscape, sources — 43 verified, decomposition — 10 typed sub-questions, prior-research fence). Wave 1: 8 single-hat cells against the pruned matrix (approved by the owner as-is). Wave 2: 4 red-team cells (stale-index verification, warm-corpus attack, matcher-fix precision on real corpora, steelman of losing candidates) plus a self-organized cross-cell adjudication chain with withdrawals and counter-measurements. Wave 3 (owner-selected): 3 measurement cells closing the ceiling questions. Runtime: Brain MCP available (brain_mode true); agent-teams implicit session team; WebSearch unavailable in some cell harnesses — disclosed transport substitutions (curl, delegated fetch subagents) accepted with source classes honored. Known process findings fed back into the wave-coordination skill note during the run: unfenced quantitative claims in briefs (C-10), background return-text delivery via final-action messages. The claim schema (contradicting_sources mandatory, dated sources, triangulation and falsification for high-confidence claims) was enforced by re-engagement; cannot-find with exhausted queries accepted as terminal. Evidence artifacts persist under skills scratch/deep-research-cells/ including the provenance/ copies of the ephemeral acceptance scripts.
