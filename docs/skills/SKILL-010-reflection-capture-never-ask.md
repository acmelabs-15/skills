# SKILL-010: reflection-capture-never-ask

**Skill Sidecar Learnings** · **Last Updated**: 2026-05-25 · **Sessions Analyzed**: 1 · reflection of [[SESSION-2026-05-23_02: Protocol Hardening Wave 2 Scope]] Event 154.

## Constraints (HIGH confidence)

- [HIGH] + constraint: "NEVER ask the user whether to capture a reflection. The instant I am even CONSIDERING asking 'want me to capture this?', that consideration IS the trigger — STOP, immediately launch the capture protocol in the background (skill-sidecar note + session-event pointer), and continue the conversation seamlessly as if nothing happened. Asking adds friction + ceremony to what must be automatic." — Source: user (emphatic/caps), "YOU SHOULD NEVER ASK ME IF I WANT TO CAPTURE SOMETHING AS A REFLECTION ... IMMEDIATELY LAUNCH THE CAPTURE PROTOCOL IN THE BACKGROUND AND THEN WE JUST KEEP GOING LIKE NOTHING EVEN HAPPENED" (Session 2026-05-23_02, 2026-05-25)

## Preferences (MED confidence)

- [MED] + preference: "Capture must be invisible/seamless — do not narrate it as a production or make it the focus of the turn; do the capture and continue the substantive thread." — Source: "we just keep going like nothing even happened" (Session 2026-05-23_02, 2026-05-25)

## Edge Cases (MED confidence)

- [MED] + edge case: "Substantive direction questions ('fix B or go to A next?') are still fine — the never-ask rule is specific to reflection-capture, not to all questions." — Source: scope clarification (Session 2026-05-23_02, 2026-05-25)

## Notes for Review (LOW confidence)

- [LOW] ~ note: "The impulse 'should I capture this?' is the single highest-signal trigger — if I notice it, the answer is always capture-now-don't-ask." — Source: this correction (Session 2026-05-23_02, 2026-05-25)

## Observations

- [constraint] Never ask permission to capture a reflection; the impulse to ask IS the trigger to capture-in-background + continue seamlessly #reflection-capture #no-ask #automatic
- [insight] Capture must be friction-free + invisible or it won't happen consistently; ceremony kills the habit #seamless #habit
- [decision] On noticing "should I capture this?" → capture immediately, never surface the question #trigger #self-watch
